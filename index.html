02 The Difference

Tooling, torque, and tenths.

Anyone can quote a part. We engineer the path to it — and put the proof in writing.

  • DFM feedback in 48 hours. Send a print or STEP file; get back a manufacturability review, not just a number.
  • First-article inspection, standard. Every new part is measured against the print before the run continues.
  • Documentation with every shipment. Material certs, inspection reports, and certificates of conformance — filed and traceable.
  • One accountable partner. Machining, fabrication, finishing, and assembly coordinated under one roof.
