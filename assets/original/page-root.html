Who we are

 Reticle Manufacturing Solutions (RMS) is a manufacturing-engineering consultancy and precision machining provider offering turnkey production solutions and mid volume production support through customer on-site delivery and in house machining capabilities

 

We specialize in integrating Lean principles, Kaizen culture, Design for Manufacturability (DFM), and engineering-driven process optimization into product development and production environments.


This approach gives customers complete visibility into their true production costs, ensuring accuracy in job profitability every time.


Our hybrid model—combining expert consulting with in-house machining—allows us to validate processes, prove out designs, and deliver turnkey solutions grounded in practical manufacturing experience.


This dual capability also enables us to directly support prototyping and mid-low volume production needs for all industries.
